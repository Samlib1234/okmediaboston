OTG.video branding mock up

The president of OTG.video asked for a logo that uses the RGB colors and that was distinctively a video production companies logo. He wanted it to be super simple so it could easily be printed on black t-shirts worn by his staff during production.This meant that the main logo could not contain any black and would have to stand out against a black background. We came up with this simple OTG with a red dot logo. The red dot is easily identifiable as a recording dot that appears on cameras once they are "rolling". The futura font is elegant yet simple and to the point, similar to his companies theme (on the go). They want to be known for a no gimmicks, to the point production company that is available on demand and produces high quality material.
