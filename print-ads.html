Storefront ad for Symphony Mart on Boylston St. This was a convenience store above my apartment that I frequented, it had snacks from all over the world... hence the ad^

1st ad I ever made

This was for a watch store right next to my school, Suffolk University, the owner fixed my watch before and i'd see the storefront everyday while walking to school. Eventually I thought about starting an advertising agency and I knew that would be the perfect place to start. It gave me credibility and visibility in Downtown Boston as an 18 year old freshman in college. Having my ad hanging on a busy street with my company name that I just made up right at the bottom made it easier to approach future clients.
